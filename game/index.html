Ember & Thyme — Sizzle at the Sunlit Hearth

A complete cooking game: take tickets, chop, master the Ember Heat gold zone, assemble, serve. Keyboard + mouse. No network.

Click a ticket on the ORDER BOARD, fetch its ingredient at the PANTRY, chop it, stop the Ember Heat needle in the GOLD zone, assemble the plate, then serve it. WASD moves the chef · E acts · 1–6 pantry · C chop · K stove · X discard · M mute · R restart.
